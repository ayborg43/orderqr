Run the demo

  1. Open Table 5 as a guest. Add a Smash Burger with a cooking preference, extra cheese and the note “no onion”, then a lemonade.
  2. Check out with the approving card. Note the order number and the itemised surcharges.
  3. Open the kitchen display. The burger shows under Grill and the lemonade under Drinks. Advance the order to Ready, then Served.
  4. Back as the guest, watch the status change, order a dessert, then open the table’s bill.
  5. In admin, switch a drink off and try to order it. Then try the declining card, and a double-tap on Pay.
