Frequently asked questions

What are the benefits of QR ordering?

Guests order and pay at their own pace, tables turn faster, and staff spend time on service instead of running orders back and forth. Every paid order reaches the kitchen instantly, with modifiers and notes attached.

Will I need to build an app for QR ordering?

No. Guests scan the table code and order in their phone browser: no download, no account, no login. It works for groups and families where everyone orders for themselves.

Does QR ordering reduce labour costs?

It reduces the trips, not the team. Fewer order-taking and payment runs means the same staff can look after more tables, and guests never wait just to ask for the bill.

Can you help with QR codes and in-venue promotion?

Yes: the admin dashboard generates a printable table card with a QR code for every table. Print them, stand them on tables, and you’re live.

Does QR ordering improve accuracy, or cause more mistakes?

Guests type their own modifiers and kitchen notes, availability is re-checked by the server at checkout, and double-taps can’t create duplicate orders. Fewer hand-offs, fewer mistakes.

How does this help with surcharges and bills?

Card and public-holiday surcharges are set once in admin and itemised automatically on every bill. A table’s full bill — every order and the total — is always one tap away for guests.
